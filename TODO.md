## Priority Cooking & Episode Queue

- [ ] **1. Cook Traditional Pinoy Sisig (Authentic Kapampangan Style)**
  - **Goal**: Make Sisig the traditional way using authentic ingredients and technique (no mayonnaise shortcuts).
  - **Key Traditional Elements to Source in Melbourne**:
    - Pork jowl (*pisngi*), pork ears (*tenga*), and/or snout (*maskara*) — boiled until tender, then char-grilled/broiled for smoky crispness.
    - Chicken livers (mashed/sautéed as the traditional rich binder).
    - Souring & aromatics: Fresh *calamansi* (or native vinegar), plenty of chopped red/shallot onions, *siling labuyo* (bird's eye chilies), salt, and cracked black pepper.
    - Served on a blazing-hot cast-iron sizzling plate (*plantsa*).
  - **Story / Non-Fiction Angle**: How Aling Lucing (Lucia Cunanan) in Angeles City, Pampanga reinvented an ancient sour prep (*sisig* originally meant sour/green fruit salad) along the railroad tracks into one of the world's greatest pork dishes—and what gets lost when modern recipes replace chicken liver and calamansi with mayo.
