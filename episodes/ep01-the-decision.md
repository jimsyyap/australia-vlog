# Episode 01: Why I'm Moving to Australia (Working Title)

## Packaging (Title & Thumbnail Ideas)
- **Title Idea 1**: Why I'm Leaving Everything to Move to Australia
- **Title Idea 2**: Moving to Australia in 2026: The Honest Truth (Before I Go)
- **Title Idea 3**: I'm Moving to Australia. Here's the Plan.
- **Thumbnail Concept**: Split frame or expressive close-up with packed boxes / passport + Australian map or destination city in background; short 3-word text overlay (e.g., *"GOODBYE?"* or *"AUSTRALIA BOUND"*).

---

## Hook (0:00 – 0:45)
- **Visual**: Packing a box or holding passport/visa grant letter, cutting to b-roll of current home vs. destination in Australia.
- **Talking Head / VO**: State the big news immediately, the timeline, and the central tension/question of the episode (*Why Australia? What does it actually take to pull this off?*).

---

## Story Beats / Outline
1. **The Catalyst**: Why move now? What sparked the decision?
2. **Where in Australia & Why**: Choosing the city/region and what drew you there.
3. **The Logistics & Hurdles**: Visa process, job/work setup, selling/packing belongings, costs so far.
4. **Fears vs. Excitement**: Honest reflection on what you're nervous about and what you're looking forward to most.
5. **What's Next (Call to Action)**: Tease the next episode in the series (packing up, flights, first 48 hours landing in Australia).
